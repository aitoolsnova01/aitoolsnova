What a free-tools site actually earns, in the order the money shows up. No lambo, some coffee.

Path 1 of 7

Ads (the slow one)

AdSense pays roughly $1–10 per 1,000 views depending on niche and country. Under 10k visits a month it is coffee money. That is normal — keep going.
Path 2 of 7

Affiliate, done honestly

Recommend only what you actually use. One honest comparison post out-earns ten “top 20 tools” listicles — and keeps readers coming back.
Path 3 of 7

Donations

A PayPal link costs nothing to add. Expect silence until your tool saves people real time — then a few genuinely do say thanks.
Path 4 of 7

Premium extras

Charge for the annoying-to-build parts: bulk processing, API access, no queue. Paywall the core and trust dies the same week.
Path 5 of 7

Sponsorship

Becomes possible once a page ranks. Label it clearly — one hidden ad can burn the trust that took a year to earn.
Path 6 of 7

The order matters

Traffic first, ads second, affiliate third, premium last. Doing it backwards is why most tool sites earn nothing at all.
Path 7 of 7

The trap

Six ad units on one tool page → slower page → less traffic → less revenue. It is a loop. Two well-placed units beat six desperate ones.

Build the useful thing first
