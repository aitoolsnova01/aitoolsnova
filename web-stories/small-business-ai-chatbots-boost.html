Note 1 of 7

Put it on FAQs only

Train it on the 20 questions you already answer daily — hours, delivery, returns. That is 70% of chats handled without you.
Note 2 of 7

Fence the money questions

Do not let it invent refund policy. Give exact wording for anything involving money, or make it hand off. Ours once offered a discount we never had.
Note 3 of 7

One-tap human handoff

“Talk to a person” must be one tap away. A bot loop at 9pm loses exactly the customer you built the bot to catch.
Note 4 of 7

The real win is 2am

Daytime chat volume looks good in reports. The actual value is the after-hours enquiry captured with a name and number.
Note 5 of 7

Test it every Monday

Ask it your top ten questions weekly. Menus and prices change; the bot will not know until you retrain it.
Note 6 of 7

Complaints skip the bot

Angry customers get angrier with a bot. Route complaint keywords straight to a human — that one rule saves reviews.
Note 7 of 7

Watch the free-tier cap

Free plans cap chats per month. Fine for a quiet shop; a busy store hits the cap and the widget goes dark mid-month.
