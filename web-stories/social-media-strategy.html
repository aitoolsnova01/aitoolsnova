Posting daily on five platforms burned us out in six weeks. This is the schedule that survived.

Rule 1 of 7

Pick 1–2 platforms

Be where your customers scroll, not where marketers say to be. A plumber gets leads from Google and Facebook, not TikTok.
Rule 2 of 7

Batch on Sunday

Two hours, five posts, all scheduled. Improvising a post every morning is how streaks — and enthusiasm — die.
Rule 3 of 7

AI drafts, you finish

Ask AI for ten hook options, then rewrite the winner in your own words. Raw AI captions read like everyone else’s feed.
Rule 4 of 7

Mostly useful, rarely selling

Teach or entertain most of the time; sell occasionally. Feeds that are all offers get muted, not unfollowed — you never see it happen.
Rule 5 of 7

Native beats reposted

The same video uploaded natively outperforms a crossposted link almost every time. Annoying, but consistently true.
Rule 6 of 7

Count what pays

Track saves, shares and replies — they signal buyers. Likes and follower counts feel good and pay nothing.
Rule 7 of 7

Skip bad-fit trends

If the trend does not fit your product, skip it. A tax firm doing a dance trend converts nobody and haunts the grid forever.
