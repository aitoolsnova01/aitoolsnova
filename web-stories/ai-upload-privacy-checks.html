10 Privacy Checks Before Any AI Upload

Take 60 seconds to protect your data before you press Upload.

Tip 1 of 10

1. Classify the file first

Public files are usually low risk. Personal files need redaction, while confidential documents belong in an approved or offline tool.
Tip 2 of 10

2. Prefer local processing

Choose tools that work inside your browser when possible. Your device can resize, compress or convert without uploading the original file.
Tip 3 of 10

3. Remove extra details

Crop faces, hide account numbers and delete metadata before upload. Share only the minimum information required to complete the task.
Tip 4 of 10

4. Check deletion terms

Search the privacy policy for retain, delete and storage. Trust a specific deletion period instead of a vague promise about temporary files.
Tip 5 of 10

5. Ask about AI training

“We do not sell data” does not prevent model training. Verify whether your exact plan excludes uploads or provides a training opt-out.
Tip 6 of 10

6. Verify the real domain

Check spelling, HTTPS and the company identity before uploading. A padlock encrypts the connection but does not prove the operator is trustworthy.
Tip 7 of 10

7. Review third parties

Cloud storage, analytics and model providers may also process your file. Check the sub-processors and the countries where data can travel.
Tip 8 of 10

8. Secure your account

Use a unique password and multi-factor authentication. Reject unnecessary access to email, contacts or cloud drives for a simple conversion tool.
Tip 9 of 10

9. Test a safe sample

Try a harmless sample before the valuable original. Check output quality, watermarks, public sharing links and unexpected data collection first.
Tip 10 of 10

10. Stop when unsure

Review the result before publishing. If a high-risk privacy answer is missing or unclear, redact the file, process it offline or do not upload.
