What actually moved a small site from zero to indexed — in the order it happened, months included.

Step 1 of 7

Reality first

A new site takes months to rank, not days. If someone promises page one in two weeks, they are selling you something.
Step 2 of 7

Pick smaller fights

Search your topic first. If page one is Amazon and Wikipedia, go narrower — “best running shoes” is gone; “wide-fit trail shoes for flat feet” is not.
Step 3 of 7

One page, one job

Each page answers one search. The title states the answer; the first paragraph proves you have it. No page should chase five keywords.
Step 4 of 7

Technical basics

Sitemap submitted, readable on a phone, loads in about two seconds. For a small site, that is 90% of “technical SEO”. Stop there.
Step 5 of 7

Content

800 useful words beat 2,000 padded ones. Spam updates hit stretched AI text hard — cut filler before you hit publish.
Step 6 of 7

Links

One mention from a local paper or a niche blog beats fifty directory submissions. Earn links with something worth citing.
Step 7 of 7

Measure honestly

Install Search Console on day one. Watch impressions first, clicks later. Ignore rank trackers for the first six months.

Do the boring bits free
