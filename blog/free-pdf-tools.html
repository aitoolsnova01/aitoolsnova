Best Free PDF Tools

📅 July 10, 2026 ⏱ 5 min read 👤 AIToolsNova Team 🏷️ PDF Tools

PDFs are everywhere — resumes, reports, invoices, and e-books. But working with PDFs often requires specialized tools. Fortunately, there are excellent free PDF tools that let you convert, merge, and split PDFs without spending a penny.

💡 Quick Note: All PDF tools are completely free, work in your browser, and require no signup.

1. PDF Converter

Convert Word documents, text files, and images to PDF instantly with PDF Converter. Supports DOC, DOCX, TXT, JPG, and PNG. Perfect for creating professional PDFs from any document.

2. PDF Merger

Combine multiple PDFs into one file with PDF Merger. Ideal for merging invoices, reports, or chapters into a single document.

3. PDF Splitter

Extract specific pages from a PDF or split by range using PDF Splitter. Great for extracting individual pages from large documents.

When to Use Each Tool

  • PDF Converter: When you need to create a PDF from a Word document or image.
  • PDF Merger: When you have multiple PDFs that should be combined.
  • PDF Splitter: When you need to extract specific pages from a large PDF.

Benefits of Using Free PDF Tools

  • No Software Installation: Works entirely in your browser.
  • Fast Processing: Convert and merge files in seconds.
  • Privacy: Your files are processed securely and not stored.
  • Free Forever: No hidden charges or limits.
