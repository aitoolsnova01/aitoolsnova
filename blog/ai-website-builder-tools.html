What You Get in the First Ten Minutes, and What Comes After

AI builders are genuinely fast at the blank-page problem. Describe the business and you get a structured site with plausible sections, which is a better starting point than an empty canvas and removes the week most people lose to choosing a template.

The generated copy is placeholder quality regardless of how polished it looks. It describes a generic business in your category, and visitors can tell. Replace the hero, the about section and every call to action with specifics before launch.

Check the mobile rendering immediately. Most of these tools generate desktop-first and adapt afterwards, and the adaptation frequently produces oversized hero text and stacked navigation that pushes content below two screens.

Lock-in, Performance and the Exit Cost

Ask how you leave before you commit. Some builders export clean HTML, others export nothing usable, and discovering that after two years of accumulated content and rankings is an expensive lesson. Portability is worth paying for even if you never use it.

Watch page weight. Generated sites tend to ship large hero images, several font families and animation libraries you did not ask for. That combination is what turns a good-looking site into one that loses mobile visitors before it paints.

SEO Fundamentals These Builders Frequently Miss

Check the generated title tags and meta descriptions page by page. AI builders commonly produce the same title across the whole site or leave the descriptions empty, and both quietly cap how the site performs in search regardless of how good the pages look.

Verify the heading structure too. Generated layouts often use heading tags for visual sizing, so a page ends up with four separate top-level headings and no logical hierarchy. It is invisible to visitors and confusing to search engines and screen readers alike.

Confirm that URLs are clean and stable before launch. Some builders generate paths with numeric suffixes or change them when you rename a page, which breaks links and loses whatever ranking that page had accumulated.

What to Look For When Choosing A website builder

  • Export or self-host option. If your site only exists inside their platform, migrating later is painful and expensive.
  • Real page speed. Generated sites often ship heavy scripts. Test the published URL, not the editor preview.
  • SEO control. You need editable titles, descriptions, canonical tags and a sitemap. Many builders hide these.
  • Custom domain on the plan you will use. Some free tiers force a subdomain, which limits credibility and search performance.

Mistakes That Cost Small businesses and creators Time

Accepting placeholder content. Generated filler text left live is a common and very visible mistake.

Ignoring mobile. Most traffic is mobile. Check every page at phone width before launch.

No analytics from day one. Without measurement you cannot tell whether anything is working.

More Questions Answered

Are AI-built websites good for SEO?

They can be, provided you control titles, descriptions, headings and page speed. Problems come from bloated templates rather than from AI itself.

Can I move my site to another host later?

Only if the builder allows export. Check this before you invest weeks of content work.

Do I need to know how to code?

No for a standard site. Custom functionality usually still requires a developer.

Is the free plan enough?

For a simple presence, sometimes. A custom domain and removal of platform branding almost always need a paid plan.
