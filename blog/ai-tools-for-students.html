Frequently Asked Questions

Are the tools covered in "Best AI Tools for Students in 2026" free to use?

Most of the tools featured here offer a genuinely free tier that covers everyday use. Some also sell paid plans with higher limits or extra features, so always check the provider's current pricing page before you commit.

Do I need any technical skill to get started?

No. These tools are built for beginners — you work in your browser, and most tasks are a matter of typing a prompt or uploading a file. If you can use a word processor, you can use these.

Do these tools work on mobile?

Yes, the browser-based options work on Android and iOS. For heavier tasks like long-form editing or bulk exports, a laptop is usually more comfortable.
