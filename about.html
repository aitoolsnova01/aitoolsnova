About AIToolsNova: AI Tools for Society

Your one-stop destination for 100+ free AI tools, SEO tools, image tools, PDF tools and online utilities.

Our Story

Hi — we're the small team behind AIToolsNova. Like a lot of people, we kept hitting the same wall: every "free" online tool we found either asked for an email before it would do anything, capped us after three uses, or quietly signed us up for a subscription we never wanted.

So we built the site we wished already existed. One place where you can open a tool, use it, and get on with your day — no account, no trial, no "upgrade to unlock." It started as a handful of calculators and has grown into the library of 100+ tools you see today.

Our Vision and Mission

We believe AI should help people participate, learn and solve everyday problems—not make them feel left behind. Our vision is a society where technology widens access while human judgement, privacy and local knowledge remain essential.

Most "free" tools aren't actually free — you pay with your email address, your data, or a subscription you forget to cancel. We do it differently. Every tool here is free, most run entirely in your browser, and none of them ask you to create an account. That's the whole point, and we'll say it plainly:

  • Completely Free — No signup, no subscription, no hidden charges. Ever.
  • Privacy-First — We never store your files or data permanently. Your privacy matters to us.
  • Fast & Reliable — Optimized for speed on every device, from desktop to mobile.
  • Always Accessible — Available 24/7 from anywhere in the world with an internet connection.

💡 Our Promise: We will never charge for our core tools. We're committed to keeping AIToolsNova free forever.

Why Trust Us

We're a small team, and we use these tools ourselves. Before anything goes live, we actually open it and run it in a normal browser — not in a polished demo. If a tool needs a heavy server to work properly, we tell you that up front instead of faking a download button.

We also say what a tool can't do. AI-generated text can be confidently wrong, and our PDF converter turns images into PDFs rather than pretending to convert Word files without a server. We'd rather be honest than look impressive.

Editorial standards

AIToolsNova publishes practical guides about free AI tools, SEO utilities and online productivity software. Our editorial approach:

  • Hands-on testing: We prefer workflows we can reproduce in a browser without paid accounts.
  • No fake claims: We do not invent traffic numbers, guaranteed income or medical/financial promises.
  • Clear monetisation: The site is free because of advertising (Google AdSense) and occasional affiliate links marked with rel="sponsored".
  • Corrections: If something is outdated, email aitoolsnova01@gmail.com and we will update the page.
