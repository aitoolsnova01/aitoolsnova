🎂 Age Calculator

Find your exact age in years, months and days — completely free.

What is the Age Calculator?

Age Calculator is a free tool that tells you exactly how old you are — not just in years, but in months, days, weeks, even hours. It also counts down the days until your next birthday, which is handy when you're filling a form that asks for your age "as of" a specific date.

💡 Key Feature: Get your age in years, months and days, plus total days lived and a next-birthday countdown — completely free, nothing is stored.

How to Use the Age Calculator

  1. Enter your date of birth in the first box.
  2. (Optional) Pick an "as of" date if you need your age on a past or future date — for a school admission or visa form, for example.
  3. Click "Calculate Age" and read your result.

Why People Use This Tool

  • Exact age: Know your age to the day instead of guessing.
  • Form filling: Get age "as of" a deadline for admissions, exams or job applications.
  • Age difference: Work out the gap between two dates in a moment.
  • Fun facts: See total days, weeks and hours you've been alive.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: Is the Age Calculator free?

Yes, it's completely free with no limits and no signup.

Q2: Does it work for any date?

Yes. You can enter any past date as the "as of" date to find your age on that day.

Q3: Is my birth date stored anywhere?

No. The calculation runs entirely in your browser and nothing is saved or sent to a server.

Q4: How is the result calculated?

It compares your birth date with the "as of" date, adjusting for month lengths and leap years so the day count is exact.
