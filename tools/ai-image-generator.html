Best Prompt Formula for AI Images

A great AI prompt usually follows this structure:

[Subject] + [Action / Scene] + [Style] + [Lighting] + [Camera / Detail] + [Quality tags]

Example: "A young woman holding a coffee cup (subject) walking down a rainy Tokyo street at night (scene), cinematic anime style (style), neon reflections on wet asphalt (lighting), shot on 35mm film (camera), ultra detailed, 8k (quality)".

Paid tools like Midjourney (Discord-only, ~$10/mo), DALL·E 3 (limited free tier via ChatGPT) and Stable Diffusion (requires GPU or paid host) all produce great images — but they need accounts, subscriptions or setup.
