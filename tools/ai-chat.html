Getting Better Answers From Any Chat Model

Give the model a role and a goal in the same breath. 'You are a tax accountant explaining to a first-time freelancer' produces a different and usually far more useful answer than the same question asked cold, because it constrains vocabulary, depth and assumptions all at once.

Iterate instead of restarting. When an answer misses, reply with what specifically was wrong rather than rewriting the question from scratch. Conversations converge on what you wanted; repeated cold starts circle around it.

Ask for the reasoning when the answer matters. Requesting the steps or the assumptions makes errors visible, and it is far easier to spot a wrong assumption than a wrong conclusion presented confidently.

Knowing the Limits Before They Cost You

Models have a training cutoff and cannot check anything after it unless they are actively searching the web. Pricing, product features, current events and anything regulatory are the areas where confident but outdated answers cause real problems.

Long conversations drift. Details from early on get progressively less weight, so for anything precise it is better to restate the key constraints than to assume they are still being honoured twenty messages later.

Never paste anything you would not send to a stranger. Free tiers commonly use conversations for training, and once submitted there is no practical way to retract it.

What this chat is good at — and where it isn't

Treat it like a fast colleague, not an oracle. It's strong at explaining concepts, rewriting a clumsy paragraph, drafting replies, and unsticking you when you don't know how to start. It's weak at anything that changed recently, exact prices, and niche local facts — for those, verify before you act.

Getting better answers

  • Give context first: "I run a two-person bakery in Leeds" changes the answer more than any clever wording.
  • Ask for the format you want: "as a checklist", "under 100 words", "explain like I'm new to this".
  • If the first answer is generic, reply "be specific — give numbers and an example". It usually complies.
  • Don't paste passwords, client contracts, or unpublished exam material into any chat box, including this one.

Honest limits

Responses come from external AI providers, so at peak times a reply can take several seconds or occasionally fail — resend once before assuming it's broken. Long conversations lose the thread; start fresh for a new topic. For long-form articles, the AI Writer is set up better for structure.
