🎯 ATS Resume Checker

Paste your resume and the job description. Get an ATS score, the keywords you're missing, and concrete fixes — free, private, and instant.

What this checker looks at

It scores seven things real Applicant Tracking Systems and recruiters care about: contact information, standard section headings, length, quantified achievements, strong action verbs, parse-safe formatting, and — when you add a job description — keyword match against the ad.

How to get a higher score

Use standard headings (Summary, Work Experience, Education, Skills), put numbers in your bullets, start bullets with action verbs, and mirror the job ad's exact vocabulary for skills you genuinely have. Keep it to one or two pages and skip tables, icons and photos.

Q: Is it free?

Yes — unlimited checks, no signup.

Q: Is my resume uploaded?

No. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is sent to a server.

Q: What's a good score?

80+ excellent, 65-79 good. Below 65, apply the suggestions first.
