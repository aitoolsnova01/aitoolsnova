How to use these ChatGPT prompts effectively

Prompts are only as good as the brief you give. Replace bracketed placeholders with your product, audience, and constraint. Ask for structure first (outline), then expand section by section. Always fact-check names, prices and legal claims before publishing.

Prompt formula that works in the US, UK, Canada and global teams

  1. Role: who the model should act like.
  2. Task: the exact deliverable.
  3. Audience: region, industry, reading level.
  4. Constraints: length, tone, forbidden claims.
  5. Output format: bullets, table, email, script.
